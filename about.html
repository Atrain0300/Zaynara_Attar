About Zaynara

Where tradition meets modern sophistication

Every Zaynara Attar blend is a tribute to Middle Eastern perfumery heritage—refined for those who seek depth, identity, and timeless elegance.

Our Journey

Zaynara began with a simple belief: fragrance should feel personal, intimate, and soulful. Inspired by traditional attar houses, we source fine natural oils, precious woods, florals, and resins that unfold beautifully on skin.

Our perfumers balance heritage techniques with contemporary elegance, crafting compositions that are alcohol-free, long-lasting, and graceful in every setting—from quiet mornings to unforgettable evenings.
